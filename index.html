Computational Vexillology

Decoding National Aesthetics Through Data Science

By Alejandro Treny

This project applies data science techniques (color theory, geometric complexity, and regional clustering) to analyze the aesthetic design choices of 200+ global national flags.

Key Takeaways

  • Regional Patterns: Dominance of specific color palettes across continents.
  • Complexity Mapping: Quantifying geometric density in national symbols.
  • Cluster Analysis: Grouping flags by aesthetic similarity rather than geography.
